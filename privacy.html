This notice describes the privacy posture expressed by the current static TMO Validation website and its public inquiry route.

Static website

The published TMO Validation pages do not themselves provide a TMO user-account system, payment form, or site-hosted inquiry form.

External services

The site links to GitHub and may link to other external resources. Information submitted to an external service is subject to that service's applicable terms and privacy practices.

Public inquiries

The inquiry page can direct visitors to public GitHub Issues. Information posted to a public issue may be publicly visible.

Do not submit confidential, privileged, personal, credentials, secrets, or other sensitive information through a public issue.

Future site features

If the website later introduces forms, accounts, payments, analytics, or other data-processing functionality, this privacy information should be revised to accurately describe those features.

Questions

For a public project inquiry that does not contain sensitive information, use the Inquiries page .
