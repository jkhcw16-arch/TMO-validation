Current architecture

Evidence to constitutional analysis

TMO-LE preserves and qualifies evidence. TMO-CA applies constitutional text, authority, precedent, doctrine, standards, and qualified factual predicates without allowing evidence scores to substitute for legal analysis.

Preserved resource → provenance → LE qualification → epistemic assessment → CA doctrine + authority → CA application → CA conclusion → review / audit

TMO-LE foundation

Evidence invariants

Interpretation may reduce uncertainty only when additional evidence justifies that reduction.

LE-101

Preservation before interpretation

Preserve what was observed before deciding what it means.

LE-102

Assertion is not fact

Recording a proposition does not establish it as fact.

LE-103

Unknown is not zero or pass

Missing or unresolved information remains explicit.

LE-104

Independence is origin-based

Duplicate records do not become independent corroboration.

LE-105

Strength is provenance-bounded

A finding cannot exceed the support of its provenance.

LE-106

No manufactured temporal precision

Derived time claims require evidentiary support.

LE-107

Relations remain separate

Identity, role, attribution, causation, and authorization require separate support.

Epistemic layer

Evidentiary status without truth inflation

Epistemic status records what available evidence supports, not an unqualified declaration of truth.

Epistemic states

Qualified dimensions

Evidence weighting

Proposition-specific evidence assessment

Weight belongs to the relationship between an evidence item and a proposition. Evidence weight does not establish truth or constitutionality.

Relevance

How directly evidence bears on a proposition.

Context

Applicability to the relevant circumstances.

Source identity

Whether the particular source is sufficiently identified.

Authenticity

Whether the item is supported as what it purports to be.

Independence

Whether the evidence has an independent origin.

TMO-CA Constitutional Analysis

Legal analysis stays separate from evidentiary support

TMO-CA consumes qualified TMO-LE predicates while keeping constitutional text, authority, precedent, doctrine, application, counterargument, and conclusion independently addressable.

Qualified LE facts + constitutional provision + authority / precedent + doctrine / standard → CAApplication → CAConclusion → review disposition

Constitutional text

Text remains distinct from judicial, historical, doctrinal, and analytical interpretations.

Authority

Binding status, current treatment, jurisdiction, provenance, and evidence weight remain distinct.

Precedent

Holding, rationale, facts, scope, proposition-specific use, and later treatment remain separately traceable.

Doctrine

Elements and exceptions are represented and evaluated separately.

Application

Connects qualified factual predicates to explicit doctrine and legal authority.

Conclusion

Conclusions require ready applications and legal authority; adverse authority remains visible.

CA review dispositions

READY FOR REVIEW CONTESTED REVIEW NO MERITS DETERMINATION INDETERMINATE INVALID
Evidence score ≠ constitutionality.
VALID factual representation ≠ constitutional conclusion.
READY application ≠ merits conclusion.
READY FOR REVIEW ≠ judicial determination.
Adverse authority and uncertainty remain represented.
